# Staffing Analysis of Health Facilities

This project presents an analytical dashboard and data report exploring workforce patterns and marketing insights for **CL-Health**, a healthcare staffing company that connects medical professionals—both contractual and permanent—with healthcare facilities across the United States.

## Project Overview

The aim of this project is to **analyze healthcare staffing data** to derive actionable insights that support strategic sales and marketing decisions. Using data sourced from **Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)** and CL-Health’s internal platform, this dashboard and analysis address key questions around staffing demand, facility coverage, employee trends, and market opportunities.

---

## Business Context

**CL-Health** provides clinical staffing solutions across multiple U.S. states, connecting healthcare facilities with both permanent employees and contract staff. Understanding demand, utilization, and behavioral trends in staffing is critical to optimizing revenue, meeting client needs, and scaling operations.

---

##  Key Insights Delivered

- **Demand Analysis**: Identified the current demand for contract vs. permanent employees across healthcare facilities and its impact on **sales and staffing revenue**.
- **User Trends**: Tracked **employee signups** and workforce distribution across months and states, identifying high- and low-engagement periods.
- **Staffing Efficiency**: Visualized **resident-to-staff ratios** and monthly work hours to help facilities manage labor allocation more efficiently.
- **Marketing Opportunities**: Highlighted under-served states and facilities with unmet demand—providing data-driven **marketing recommendations** to increase CL-Health’s market share.
- **Operational Coverage**: Mapped **facilities by state** and analyzed trends in employee hours to uncover patterns in regional staffing.

---

## Dashboard Highlights

- Interactive filters for **month** and **state/city**
- Line chart comparing **contract vs. employee hours** by facility
- KPI cards showing total hours worked, employee signups, and resident census
- **Geographic heatmap** of facility distribution
- **Top states** by resident population and workforce demand
- Monthly trend lines for hours worked (2024 Q1)
